How to play

How to play

The goal

Steer your light to collect every green circle on the board. When the last one is gone, the next level starts. Today’s run is five levels. Endless keeps going until you run out of lives.

What you control

You control the glowing light. Move it with the arrow keys or W, A, S and D. With a mouse or a phone, press on the board and drag; your light glides toward your finger or pointer.

Press P or the Pause button to pause, and M or the Sound button to turn sound on or off. Before a run, choose Today’s run (the same five levels for every player today) or Endless.

What things cost

You get 3 lives for each run. Touching a red square costs one life. Lives do not come back during a run, and the run ends when all three are gone.

Today’s run is open to everyone. Endless lets you play levels 1 to 5. The full game is a one-time payment of $5 that opens every level after 5 in Endless. It unlocks in the browser you paid in. If you paid on another device, use Restore Purchase to email us the address you paid with.

How you earn

Each light is worth 10 points times the level number. Grab lights within about a second and a half of each other to build a chain, up to times 8, which multiplies your points. Clearing a level fast adds a speed bonus.

Your best scores are saved in this browser, and you can share a picture of your run. Points have no cash value. You cannot win money in this game.

What makes it hard

Red squares drift and bounce around the board, and each level adds more of them, moving faster. From level 4 a purple square chases you. From level 6 some squares grow and shrink. Getting hit breaks your chain. The squares stay still and harmless while each level’s name is on screen, and you blink safely for a moment after a hit.

Quick tip

Follow the shape the lights make, so you collect them in one smooth line and keep your chain going.

Read more about Glowgrid

Each level lays its lights out in a named pattern, such as a ring, a spiral or a zigzag, and the pattern suggests a route. Your light leaves a trail, and the whole run is drawn on the share card at the end. The circles are light and the squares with an X are static, so the shapes tell them apart as well as the colors.
